# Graphic language

Approved: neutral Chalk (#f5f5f5) and graphite (#292929); Instrument Sans for supporting UI, Literata for agent prose. The icon itself should use authored paths rather than a font dependency. Dark/reversed art is paper on graphite. No gradients, shadows, colored accents or glossy rendering.

PWA square grounds fill the whole canvas; platform masks own the outside corners. Keep important ink within the central maskable safe circle. Provide a small optical variant if details close up at 16px. A tab favicon can be a transparent mark with a dark-mode reversal. Drafts stay isolated under this pack until selection.
