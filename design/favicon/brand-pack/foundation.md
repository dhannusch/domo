# Domo icon foundation

This is a scoped consolidation of the existing product brief and the user's approved UI decisions, not a new positioning exercise.

## Facts and approvals

- Domo is a personal chief-of-staff agent, presented as one calm chat (AGENTS.md).
- The user chose the refined Soft Focus layout and the neutral Chalk palette in this chat.
- The user rejected overly generic AI-looking styling and requested an improved PWA/favicon logo.
- The existing icon is a ring around a solid disc. It can resemble a recording control or target.

## Design brief

A distinctive, modest mark for a useful everyday companion. One clear silhouette, no AI sparkles, gradients, robot faces, overlapping generic chat bubbles, or decorative rings. Work at 16px first, then as a phone home-screen icon. Keep neutral paper/charcoal and editable SVG geometry.

## Recommendations, not approvals

Explore a custom d monogram, a folded-paper symbol and a continuous conversation symbol. Three proposals will be rendered in context before any production icon is replaced. Do not mark a new symbol approved until the user selects one.

No market, competitor, trademark clearance, or legal claims are made.
