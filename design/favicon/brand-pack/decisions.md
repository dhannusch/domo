## Peer critique

A: immediately tied to Domo and readable at16px; lowercase initials are a crowded category, and the chamfer is subtle at tiny sizes. No geometry revision was needed after pixel review.

B: distinctive abstract folded note, more document-like. Its seam survived after optical enlargement; it can still look like a corner/window. Less immediately tied to the name.

C: final open arch has a clean counter and stable shorter leg at16px. It can read as an n or architecture; the quiet-place association is a creative hypothesis. The initial G-like loop was discarded before presentation.

Individual source/pixel critiques are in ../a-d/critique.md, ../b-fold/critique.md and ../c-chat/critique.md. Production assets and the icon generator remain unchanged while the user chooses a direction.

## Technical checks

SVG/XML validity and absence of scripts, raster data, gradients, filters and external references checked. Renders at16/24/32/64/128/256px in graphite and reversed paper. Browser-tab and home-screen-crop applications shown on the board.

Actual rasterized ink bounds and farthest radius are in previews/validation.json; no main-symbol ink exceeds the102.4px maskable safe radius on a256px canvas. This follows the [W3C Web App Manifest safe-zone definition](https://www.w3.org/TR/appmanifest/#icon-masks), checked live during this review. Keep full-bleed opaque backgrounds for final maskable exports. Separate favicon and apple-touch links remain relevant in [HTML link guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/link#providing_icons_for_different_usage_contexts).

Parent checked 18 direction SVGs as XML; no text/font, raster, script, filter, gradient or external reference. Graphite/paper contrast is 13.34:1 in either direction.

## Selected and installed source

The user delegated the final choice and explicitly requested commit/push. A Custom d is selected. The editable production masters are apps/web/public/icons/domo.svg and favicon.svg. Versioned PNG/HTML/manifest/worker references are generated by scripts/make-icons.ts. Manifest identity is retained. The other marks remain unselected studies.

Chrome's [current update guidance](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/improvements-to-web-app-updates) requires changed icon URLs/metadata to detect an icon update and may offer Review app update. The [PWA update guide](https://web.dev/learn/pwa/update#updating_metadata) describes iOS/iPadOS reinstall limitations. Actual OS launcher refresh timing is outside Domo's control; no claim of forced automatic replacement on every platform.
