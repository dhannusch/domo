# A · Custom d

Status: selected under the user’s delegated final choice.

A compact, bespoke lowercase **d**, with an asymmetric rounded bowl, a firm stem and one clipped top corner. It makes Domo recognizable by its name without adding an AI motif or mascot.

- **Mechanism:** one confident silhouette and one open counter. The bowl is hand-built from cubic paths; it is not a converted font glyph.
- **Mark idea:** a lowercase initial with the steadiness of a small everyday tool. The chamfer is the only distinguishing cut; it remains quiet at app size.
- **Type role:** the mark carries no font dependency. Supporting labels can use the existing Instrument Sans; the app keeps its Literata prose.
- **Palette role:** graphite `#292929` on Chalk `#f5f5f5`; reversed is paper on graphite. No secondary accent, filters or decorative rings.
- **Application:** `app.svg` for the installed app, `reversed.svg` for a dark ground, and `small.svg` for 16/24/32px tab/favicon use. A square ground lets iOS/Android own the outer mask.
- **Distinction:** more literal and immediately name-linked than the abstract Fold and Conversation directions. It is typography rather than an object metaphor.
- **Honest risk:** a lowercase d is a crowded category. The custom proportions and chamfer are modest differentiators, so this is the safest, least surprising direction rather than the most ownable one. It may also be mistaken for an audio or reading app without context.

## Geometry and tiny-size treatment

The main ink bounds are x54–196 and y52–206 in the 256-square viewBox. The shape lies within the central maskable safe circle, center (128,128), radius 102.4. Its two furthest squared stem corners are below 102px from the center; the bowl lies closer. Background paths fill the full square. No separate maskable source is necessary.

The 16px optical version expands the symbol to x2–14 and y1–15, opens the counter to roughly 5.8×5.7px, and simplifies the chamfer to 1px. Use it at 16/24/32px rather than simply shrinking the padded app artwork. The main source has an approximately 27px minimum bowl stroke (1.69px when scaled to 16), while the optical favicon strengthens that to roughly 3px.

All four SVGs have viewBox, a small path count, no external references, no raster content, no scripts, no fonts and no clipping. Pixel review of the first parent board confirmed the counter remains open at 16px and the mark is legible on both tab grounds. Use the optical source through 32px to avoid a size backstep between 24 and 32. The main padded source starts at 64px. No geometry refinement was needed after that board; the custom d is now selected for production.
