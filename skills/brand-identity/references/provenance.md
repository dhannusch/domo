# Visual provenance

For every asset, record:

- path and asset type;
- source: authored directly, user-provided, or external URL;
- creator, model, or backend;
- date and prompt/reference when generated;
- font and asset licenses;
- commercial-use posture;
- content hash when available;
- status: proposed, selected, approved, or retired;
- user decision or source evidence.

This is a record of process, not a legal opinion. Trademark similarity and copyright questions still
need appropriate human or professional review.
