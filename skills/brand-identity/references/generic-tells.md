# Generic tells

These read as generated or templated. If your work has one, change it rather than justify it, unless
the user asked for it.

## Typography and layout

- Tiny monospace "eyebrow" labels above headlines ("FIELD NOTE · 12", "TEARDOWN · 03").
- Meta strings joined with middle dots; issue numbers as decoration.
- Tracked all-caps labels everywhere.
- One italic or colored word picked out in a headline; strikethrough-and-replace gimmicks.
- Everything in identical rounded cards; the same soft shadow under everything.

## Color

- Near-black with one acid green or lime accent.
- Purple with lime or neon.
- Warm cream with terracotta or clay.
- Gradients, glows and glassy blur as decoration.

## Marks

- Literal diagrams of the idea: bars, arrows, flowcharts, nodes and lines, pipelines, code brackets,
  gears, checkmarks.
- A generic symbol on a rounded-square app-icon tile, used for everything.
- Abstract "momentum" swooshes and overlapping translucent shapes.
- A mark that only makes sense after the explanation.

## Process

- Three directions that are the same layout recolored.
- A board where everything is too small to judge.
- Presenting before looking at your own render.
