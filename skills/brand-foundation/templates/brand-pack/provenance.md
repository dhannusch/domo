# Asset provenance

The machine-readable companion is `manifest.json`. Keep this file human-readable for important
exceptions, approvals, and rights questions.

## Rules

- Do not ship an asset without a source or an explicit `authored-directly` entry.
- Record the exact font family and license used in a wordmark or application.
- Record the model, backend, prompt/reference, and license for generated imagery.
- Mark trademark checks advisory and unresolved until reviewed.
